Privacy policy

Effective September 21, 2026. AddPlates is run by [COMPANY NAME, LLC], called "we" below.

The short version. AddPlates works without an account, and then everything stays on your phone. If you sign in to sync, we keep your email address and one copy of your app data so it can follow you to other devices. We do not sell it, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking. You can delete your account and that copy from inside the app at any time.

WhatWhenWhy

We use your data only to run the app for you. We do not sell it, rent it, share it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

3. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings, My Account, Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in and your online copy straight away. You choose whether to also erase the data on your phone.
  • Deleted data may remain for a short time in our providers' routine backups before those are overwritten.
  • If you stop using the app without deleting your account, we keep your account until you delete it or ask us to.
  • Signing out keeps your online copy. Data on your phone is yours: clearing it, or deleting the app, removes it.

4. Your choices and rights

  • See or export your data: Settings, Data & Export gives you a file with everything the app holds.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app, including past workouts.
  • Delete it: as in section 3, or email us.
  • Stop syncing: sign out, or never sign in.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Settings at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your data. Email us and we will help, whatever the law where you are.

5. Consumer health data

Some laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, treat information about your body and exercise as health data. In AddPlates that means your workouts, body measurements and recovery ratings.

  • What we collect: only what you enter yourself, listed in section 1.
  • Why: to show you your training and progress and to suggest your next workout, and, if you sign in, to sync it.
  • Who we share it with: only Supabase, which stores it for us (section 2). We do not share it with anyone else and we never sell it.
  • Your rights: to see it, delete it and withdraw your consent, as in sections 3 and 4.

AddPlates is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

6. Security

Synced data travels over encrypted connections and is stored so that each account can read only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a sign-in email that only you can open.

7. Children

AddPlates is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you in the app first.
